# ⚖️ Equilibrium // Complete App Walkthrough & Guide

Welcome to **Equilibrium**, the native macOS study companion built for International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma Economics students (SL & HL).

---

## 🧭 Navigation & Section Breakdown

### 1. 🎛️ The Equilibrium Command Center (Bento Hero Overview)
The launchpad displays your live economic syllabus progress:
- **Economic Mastery Telemetry**: Real-time tracking across all 4 units with visual mastery rings and remaining syllabus hours.
- **4-Unit Syllabus Sidebar**: Instant navigation across:
  - *Unit 1*: Introduction to Economics & Economic Thought
  - *Unit 2*: Microeconomics (Markets, Elasticities, Market Failure)
  - *Unit 3*: Macroeconomics (AD/AS, Inflation, Unemployment, Monetary/Fiscal Policy)
  - *Unit 4*: The Global Economy (Trade, Protectionism, Exchange Rates, Balance of Payments)
- **Global Jelly Spotlight (`⌘K`)**: Search across all economic concepts, formulas, diagram models, and economic theorists.

### 2. 🌊 Continuous Flow Policy Stream (`Stream` View)
- Detailed lecture notes breaking down economic theory, real-world case studies, and quantitative calculations.
- Evaluative frameworks using **CLASPP** (Consumers, Low-income earners, Allocative efficiency, Society, Producers, Public finances).

### 3. 🧩 Living Diagram Lab & Concept Matrix (`Matrix` View)
- Interactive diagrams for PPC shifts, Market Equilibrium, Negative Externalities of Consumption with Pigouvian taxes, Classical vs. Keynesian AD/AS gaps, and Tariffs with deadweight loss.
- Visual step-by-step curve shift rules and examiner diagram annotations.

### 4. ⚡ Active Recall & Calculation Cram (`Revision` View)
- Flashcards covering formulas: Price Elasticity of Demand (PED), Cross Elasticity (XED), Keynesian Multiplier, Real GDP, Consumer Price Index (CPI), and Gini Coefficient.
- Paper 1 15-mark essay structure outlines and evaluation rubrics.

### 5. 📚 Economists Vault & Quantitative Bank (`Glossary` View)
- Profiles of foundational economic thinkers (Adam Smith, Keynes, Hayek, Friedman, Piketty, Ostrom).
- Comprehensive formula reference with unit definitions and calculation examples.

---

## ⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts & Power Features
- `⌘ + K`: Open Global Jelly Spotlight Search
- `⌘ + B`: Toggle Navigation Sidebar for wide monitor view
- `⌘ + I`: Open Right Inspector Drawer (Diagram checklists & scoring rubrics)
- `⌘ + E`: Launch Timed Exam Sprint (5m, 15m, 45m essay planning drill)
- `Space`: Flip active recall card in Revision Mode
- `⌘ + 1`: Switch to Continuous Flow Mode
- `⌘ + 2`: Switch to Active Recall Cram Mode
